# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

First version, in testing.

- **Tuner:** needle gauge or strobe, standard and alternate tunings including custom ones, adjustable A4 reference, pitch graph and an intonation check.
- **Metronome:** 2, 3, 4 or 6 beats per bar with accent and mute per beat, subdivisions, tap tempo, a speed trainer, gap bars, polyrhythm pulses, beat flash and haptics, and a practice timer with a daily goal.
- **Chords:** a voicing library with diagrams, a circle-of-fifths chord dial, saved progressions with playback, scales on the fretboard, and Jam backing tracks.
- **Practice:** guided routines, ear training and fretboard note drills, with weekly stats.
- **Learn:** short lessons with quizzes.
- Layouts for phones and tablets in portrait and landscape; English, French and Vietnamese.
